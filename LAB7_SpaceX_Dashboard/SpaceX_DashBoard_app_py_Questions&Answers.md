**Ques 1: Which site has the largest successful launches?**



KSC LC-39A. If you look at your "All Sites" pie chart, KSC LC-39A takes up the largest slice at 41.7% of total successes.



**Ques 2: Which site has the highest launch success rate?**



KSC LC-39A. If you select each site individually from the dropdown, you will see that KSC LC-39A has 10 successes out of 13 launches (about a 76.9% success rate).



**Ques 3: Which payload range(s) has the highest launch success rate?**



The 2,000 kg to 4,000 kg range. On the scatter plot, this section is heavily clustered with successful (class 1) launches.



**Ques 4: Which payload range(s) has the lowest launch success rate?**



The 0 to 2,000 kg range (and beyond 6,000 kg), where you will see a higher density of failed (class 0) launches.



**Ques 5: Which F9 Booster version has the highest launch success rate?**



The FT version has the highest total number of successful launches across all weights, while the B5 version boasts a 100% success rate (though it has very few total launches in this dataset).
